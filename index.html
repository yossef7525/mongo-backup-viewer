Mongo Backup Viewer

Open a MongoDB mongodump archive (.gz, BSON) directly in your browser, browse databases and collections, filter documents with MongoDB queries and export the result to JSON, Extended JSON or JSON Lines. Everything runs locally, so your backup never leaves your device.

  • Browse databases, collections and indexes
  • Filter with MongoDB find queries: filter, projection, sort, skip, limit
  • Export to JSON, Extended JSON (mongoimport-compatible) and JSON Lines
  • 10 languages, including right-to-left Hebrew and Arabic

How to create a compatible backup

mongodump --archive=backup.gz --gzip

Privacy

Files are processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no analytics or third-party request.
